Render assigns a shared, dynamic IP, not a dedicated one. The same
hostname has been seen in 216.24.57.0/24 and in the 74.220.48.0/24 /
74.220.56.0/24 ranges, so the address must never be hard-coded in a hosts
file, DNS record or allowlist. Reference the hostname instead.
